5. Customer journey (explain this simply)

  1. Customer clicks your link → Lemon Squeezy checkout
  2. Pays with card (Lemon Squeezy handles payment & receipt)
  3. Goes to My account at studio9medical.com/conta/
  4. Clicks Continue with Google — same email address as checkout
  5. Sees the purchased module under My packages → clicks Open
  6. Studies in the module app (Biology or Genetics)

Alternative: if they don’t use Google, they can choose Use email link on the same page (check spam folder).
