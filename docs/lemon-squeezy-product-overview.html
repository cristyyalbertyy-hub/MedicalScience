1. Product overview

Studio9 Medical Science is a digital education platform for health-sciences students (medicine, nursing, biomedical sciences, and related fields).

Each module is a self-contained online study package delivered through a web app. Students purchase access to one or more modules and study at their own pace.

What each module includes (per topic)

  • Short video lessons
  • Podcast versions for mobile/revision
  • Infographics and visual summaries
  • Practice questions (interactive quizzes)

Additional features

  • 1-year online access after purchase (no subscription)
  • Passwordless login via Google Sign-In at My account (same email as checkout); email link available as fallback
  • Progress tracking across topics and resources
  • Responsive web apps — works on phone, tablet, and desktop

Currently available for purchase (pilot)

  • Medical Biology — cell biology, molecular processes, cancer biology (19 topics)
  • Genetics — basic genetics, inheritance models, clinical application (10 topics)
