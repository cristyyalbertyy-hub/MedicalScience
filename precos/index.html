Quick questions

FAQ.

Can I really study at the beach?

Absolutely — on the beach, in the park, on the bus, at the bar. Videos, podcasts and infographics are made to be absorbed in a relaxed way: you learn while life happens, not only when you are chained to a desk.

Are the teachers real?

Yes. Cristina Alberto, university professor with 30 years of experience, and Alexandra Sousa, business consultant with 20 years of experience, shape the academic vision of the course.

Does this replace university?

No. It is a complement to help you study in a lighter and more efficient way. The topics follow the common health-sciences curriculum.

Can I get a refund?

No. All sales are final. Each module includes 12 months of digital access from purchase.

Where can I find further study suggestions?

Each live Studio9 app includes a Further study section on the course overview — books we recommend when you want to go deeper after a lesson. They support your own reading alongside university; they do not replace your official programme bibliography.

Should I buy a module or take Studio9 Pass?

Buy a module if you only need one discipline. Choose Studio9 Pass if you will study several subjects in the same semester — you unlock all paid modules while the subscription is active, then cancel when the term ends.

Does the Pass include a 24/7 tutor?

No. Pass is continuous access to the full module library, progress tools and updates — not private tutoring. For account or study questions we reply by email within 24 hours.

What is Studio9 Snap?

A Pass-only timed game: a prompt appears and you snap it onto the right answer before the clock runs out. Each live standalone module has its own weekly ranking. Package buyers study the module; Pass subscribers also get Snap.
