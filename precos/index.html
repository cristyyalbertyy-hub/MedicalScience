Quick questions

FAQ.

Can I really study at the beach?

Yes. The app works offline. Save the videos and podcasts before leaving home.

How do I receive the certificate?

After completing a module (with exercises above 80%), you receive a downloadable PDF with a verification code.

Are the teachers real?

Yes. The modules were designed by a board of 5 teachers from medical and health science faculties.

What if I do not like it?

We refund you in the first 7 days if you complete less than 20% of the module. No questions asked.

Does this replace university?

No. It is a complement to help you study in a lighter and more efficient way. The topics follow the common curriculum from Year 1 to Year 3 of health sciences.
