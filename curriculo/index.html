Full curriculum · Medical Science

Three years, 22 modules. Each module = 40 to 60 hours of study. Go at your own pace.

Year 1 · Foundations 6 modules
Subject Hours Brief description
Biology and Genetics 55h

Cell biology, molecular genetics, inheritance, and the foundations of clinical genetics

Chemistry and Introductory Biochemistry 50h

Atoms, molecules, bonds, pH, and core biochemical pathways for health sciences

Economics and International Social Politics 40h

Health systems, global policy, and the social context of medicine and public health

Histology and Embryology 45h

Microscopy, fundamental tissues, early development, and correlation with anatomy

Human Anatomy I 60h

Skeletal, muscular and nervous systems – the base for clinical study. Studio9 package (live) →

Physics, Statistics and Information Processing 45h

Physical principles in medicine, data literacy, and tools for evidence-based study

Year 2 · Systems and disease 8 modules
Subject Hours Brief description
Medical Genetics 55h

Mendelian inheritance, mutations, genetic disorders, gene therapy

Human Anatomy II 55h

Cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, urinary and reproductive systems

Physiology II 55h

Renal, digestive, endocrine and reproductive systems

Immunology 50h

Adaptive immunity, vaccines, allergies, autoimmunity

Microbiology 50h

Bacteria, viruses, fungi, parasites – pathogenesis and diagnosis

Pharmacology I 45h

Pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, CNS and cardiovascular drugs

Epidemiology 40h

Frequency measures, study designs, screening

Biostatistics 40h

Descriptive and inferential statistics for health sciences

Year 3 · Clinical integration 8 modules
Subject Hours Brief description
Systemic Pathology 55h

System pathology (heart, lung, kidney, liver)

Clinical Medicine I 50h

Basic semiology, common symptoms, clinical reasoning

Clinical Medicine II 50h

Common conditions: hypertension, diabetes, asthma, infections

Pharmacology II 50h

Drugs for infection, cancer and metabolic diseases

Medical Ethics and Law 30h

Consent, confidentiality, beginning and end of life

Evidence-Based Practice 40h

How to read papers, use guidelines and apply them to decisions

Health Psychology 40h

Treatment adherence, clinician-patient communication, burnout

Integrated Cases 40h

Cross-system clinical cases: from symptoms to treatment
